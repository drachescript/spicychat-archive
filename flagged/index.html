Guideline review

An unlisted, publicly accessible research page for possible guideline concerns in current public or previously verified-restored bots. A keyword signal is not a proven violation or a finding of illegal activity.

No moderation decisions. Nothing here deletes, blocks, demotes, modifies, or reports a bot. Original archive pages remain available. Human review is required, especially for realistic images and context-dependent topics.

Checks are voluntary and on-demand. Normal discovery uses the existing public Typesense listing index (no Archive R2 bot reads). Including historical restorations fetches one existing compact index, not individual bot files. Results are samples, not a complete audit of the archive.
