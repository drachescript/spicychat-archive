What this site is for

Keep a useful history of public SpicyChat content, especially when it disappears.

The archive records publicly discoverable SpicyChat data over time. When something disappears, changes, or later returns, the site keeps that history instead of replacing the old state.

Archive v2 is also being designed to separate normal browsing from technical crawler information: public-facing pages should be simple to understand, while scan coverage and archive-health details stay available for people who want them.
